Where do I find my Plex token?

In Plex Web, open any item's menu, choose Get Info, then View XML. The address of the page that opens ends with X-Plex-Token= followed by your token. Treat it like a password: it can read your Plex account. This plugin only reads your libraries and never shows the token again.
